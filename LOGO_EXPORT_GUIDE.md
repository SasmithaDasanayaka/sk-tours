# SK Thai Tours Logo Export Guide

## 🚀 Quick Start - Export in One Click!

**The easiest way to export your logo:**

1. Open the file **`logo-export.html`** in your web browser (double-click it)
2. Click the **"Download PNG"** button for the platform you need:
   - **WhatsApp Business:** 640x640px
   - **Facebook Page:** 512x512px  
   - **High Resolution:** 1024x1024px (for print/marketing)
3. Upload the downloaded PNG file to your platform

That's it! No online tools needed. ✨

## 📱 Platform-Specific Instructions

### WhatsApp Business
**Requirements:** Square image, 640x640px PNG recommended

**Steps:**
1. Open `logo-export.html` in your browser
2. Click **"Download PNG"** under "WhatsApp Business"
3. On your phone:
   - Open WhatsApp Business
   - Go to **Settings → Business Settings → Profile**
   - Tap on your profile photo
   - Select **Gallery** and choose the downloaded logo
   - Save

### 📘 Facebook Page
**Requirements:** Minimum 180x180px (recommended 512x512px or higher)

**Steps:**
1. Open `logo-export.html` in your browser
2. Click **"Download PNG"** under "Facebook Page"  
3. On Facebook:
   - Go to your Facebook Page
   - Click on your profile picture
   - Select **Upload Photo**
   - Choose the downloaded logo
   - Position and save

### 📸 Instagram
**Requirements:** Square image, 320x320px minimum

**Steps:**
1. Use the WhatsApp version (640x640px) from `logo-export.html`
2. On Instagram app:
   - Go to your profile
   - Tap **Edit Profile**
   - Tap **Change Profile Photo**
   - Select the downloaded logo

### 🐦 Twitter/X
**Requirements:** 400x400px recommended

**Steps:**
1. Use the Facebook version (512x512px) from `logo-export.html`
2. On Twitter/X:
   - Go to your profile
   - Click **Edit profile**
   - Click on profile picture to upload
   - Choose the downloaded logo

## 🎨 Alternative Export Methods

### Method 1: Using Online Converters
If you need custom sizes:

1. Use **`public/logo.svg`** (in your project folder)
2. Upload to these free tools:
   - **CloudConvert:** https://cloudconvert.com/svg-to-png
   - **Convertio:** https://convertio.co/svg-png/
   - **SVG to PNG:** https://svgtopng.com/
3. Set your desired dimensions
4. Download PNG

### Method 2: macOS Preview (Mac users only)
1. Right-click `public/logo.svg`
2. Open with **Preview** app
3. **File → Export → Format: PNG**
4. Set your desired resolution
5. Save

### Method 3: Design Software
For professional editing:
- **Adobe Illustrator** - Open SVG directly
- **Figma** - Import SVG file
- **Inkscape** (Free) - Edit and export at any size

3. **Use the exact recommended sizes** for each platform
4. **Keep a backup** of your original SVG file
