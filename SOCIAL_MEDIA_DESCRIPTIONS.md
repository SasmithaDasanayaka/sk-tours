## Tips for WhatsApp Business Setup:

1. **Business Category**: Travel Agency / Tour Operator
2. **Business Hours**: 24/7 (or set specific hours)
3. **Address**: Add your Bangkok branch address
4. **Website**: Add your website URL
5. **Greeting Message**: 
   ```
   Hello! 👋 Welcome to SK Thai Tours! 
   
   How can we help you plan your perfect Thailand adventure today? 
   
   🌴 Bangkok | Pattaya | Phuket Packages
   💬 Tell us your travel dates and preferences!
   ```

6. **Away Message** (if needed):
   ```
   Thank you for contacting SK Thai Tours! ⏰
   
   We're currently away but will respond shortly. 
   Our Bangkok branch is open 24/7 for urgent inquiries.
   
   Meanwhile, check out our packages: [your-website-url]
   ```
